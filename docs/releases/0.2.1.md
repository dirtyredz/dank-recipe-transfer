# Dank Recipe Transfer 0.2.1 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

Hotfix for a client crash in 0.2.0 when JEI initialized after joining a world.

- Fixes `JeiTransferLookupMixin` producing invalid bytecode against JEI 19.70.0.464. Dank-aware
  JEI transfer handlers are still decorated after JEI performs its native lookup, including its
  universal-handler fallback.
- Adds a headless fast-gate test that applies all JEI and EMI client mixins to the exact pinned
  viewer jars and verifies every transformed class, without opening Minecraft.

No protocol, save-data, dependency-range or gameplay behaviour changes.
